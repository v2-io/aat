## Read this first: what surprised me, and where I think the framing needs adjusting

1. **The SOP store is a snapshot from 2026-09-30, and it came from the same coordinator as the failed attempt.** `sop/src/`, `sop/def/` and `sop/adr/` have not changed since 2026-09-30 (git). They were carved in one session by "the aat-refactored coordinator" and its fork (`sop/influx/jaw-proposal-and-feedback.md:3`), the day after that coordinator's foundation proposal. So the store is not an independent second instantiation. It is that lineage's verisectorium thinking, exported to a new corpus.
2. **Practice on this corpus kept moving after the SOP store froze, in the spec store, under Joseph's direct steering (2026-10-08 → 10-22).** Between those dates it changed in these ways:
   - Cards became the decisions. 29 rule-level ADRs were folded into their cards, and ADRs are kept only for calls that cut across many cards.
   - Each card is one MUST/MAY clause.
   - A hand-written `consequences:` field appeared, the reverse of `depends:`. That cuts against the SOP decision `record-to-decision-links-for-now` ("reverse is derived").
   - `strength:` (forced / decided / lean / provisional) replaced the Epistemic status section.
   - History moved to `*.context.md` sidecars, and Grounds became a single line.
   - New pieces: Implications, "Decisions carried", the `awaiting-implementers` tag, and declared inflections on terms.
   - New files: `TODO-MSC.md` and `CTQ-FACTORS.md`.

   All of this was proposed back to the SOP side (`sop/influx/udon-team-notes.md`). None of it has been carried into SOP records. If you want the *most recent* instantiation, the SOP records give the 09-30 skeleton and its reasoning, and the spans marked "where practice went" below give where it actually ended up. Your own note calls the SOP store "probably the most directly reusable artifact in the estate". I'd hold that loosely until you've read entries 1–4.
3. **The epistemic core is declared but has never been exercised.** No record anywhere in the store carries `verification-level:` or `evidence:` (grep finds none). `bin/` is empty, no linter exists, and no outline shows a ※ or ∂ column. Every `verification:` ladder in both `kinds.yaml` files is marked as a placeholder. The store states plainly that spec-lite's verification is mostly *authorization*, and that AAT's is the reverse: derivation and evidence, with authority as noise (`sop/influx/jaw-proposal-and-feedback.md:495-510`). So this corpus is a strong model for the decision, authority and process half, and a thin one for the derivation and evidence half, which is the half AAT most needs. The spec store's `strength:` measures revisitability. It is a different axis from verification, not an implementation of it.
4. **Two files you read whole have partly stale premises.**
   - `.int/README.md`: TODO-MSC itself lists "keeps the bytes" and "the same tree" as stale (`TODO-MSC.md:93`). Both were superseded by `adr/reserved-is-an-error.md` and `adr/forward-compat-aspiration.md`.
   - `.int/vsect-requirements-on-lite.md` was written 2026-09-30 from 13 questions read whole. Since then:
     - `@{…}` references were admitted into lite as raw-text embeds (2026-10-08), so requirement 3's "`@` is reserved" now holds only for `@name` / `@[k]`;
     - meta became "safe to evolve beyond lite", but `def/def-meta.md` says what meta covers is unsettled (requirement 4);
     - errors now stop parsing with no tree (`adr/errors-stop-parsing.md`).

   Each requirement is worth re-checking against the current cards (entry 41).
5. **For "lite as v3's record language", the closest working precedent is outside this patch.** `~/src/address-theory/` is a small store whose records are actually written in udon: `DECISIONS.ud`, `PRACTICA.ud`, `def/*.ud` term-groups, and a `LEXICON.md` generated by `bin/`. ASF itself already has a udon document, `~/src/arch/asf/msc/meta-process-review-2026-07-07/PROCESS-MAP-v0.udon`. Inside the patch, the proposal is explicit that records stay Markdown + YAML frontmatter until a lite parser exists, with field names kept to bare words (no `!`, no `@`) so a later move is cheap (`sop/influx/proposed-verisectorium.md:140-142`). There is still no lite parser.
6. **Decision-authority vocabulary is unsettled across three layers.**
   - `sop/adr/` uses the older one-field meanings.
   - `adr/TEMPLATE.md` and `adr/decision-authority.md` use the deciders / decided-by split.
   - Joseph's own 2026-10-01 definitions (sustained vs ratified) are recorded as pending (`adr/decision-authority.md:126`), and his 10-08 "no difference from the spec's perspective" may argue for dropping `decided-by` on content records altogether (`:129`).

   For AAT specifically, Joseph's line is that authority and provenance are proper for *process* decisions even in AAT (`sop/influx/jaw-proposal-and-feedback.md:122`).
7. **A tension with my brief.** You asked for neighbor spans in udon's `spec-0.10.x`. Joseph's standing guidance to lite agents is to *avoid reading* `v2/spec-0**` directly, because they are "unadjudicated, undisciplined, incomplete". He sends agents to them for dated timelines instead (`.int/STEWARD-VERBATIM.md:1000`; udon memory `older-thinking-via-agent.md`). The 2026-10-22 handoff repeats the rule for the whole of `v2/` outside the store. So I list them only as pointers, at the bottom.
