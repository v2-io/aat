# Map — ASF Volume 1 (AAT core) and where Volumes 2–4 lean on its roots

*Span-finder map, written 2026-10-28 by a finder agent (Claude Opus 5.5) for the AAT v3 coordinator. It lists files and line spans to read, in priority order, for a from-the-foundation rebuild of AAT. It is not a summary and not a whitelist: lower entries are still worth reading, and the order is the information. All paths are relative to `~/src/arch/asf/` unless they are absolute. Line numbers are as of ASF `HEAD` 4ee2b895 (Volume 1 was last touched 2026-08-24, commit 5ade664d). The ASF tree was treated as read-only throughout.*

## Read this first: surprises, and where I think the brief's framing could shift

1. **A Part I "root" is usually a short body sitting on a long tail of audit material.** Part I segment bodies run 25–150 lines. In most of them, everything after `## Working Notes` is "Incidental audit gold (lift 2026-05-30)": ideas collected from roughly 14 de novo audits. Within that gold, the `Follow-up items` subsection is where fresh readers recorded structural doubts about the root itself. The other subsections (Brief prose, Readers often ask, figures, Belongs elsewhere) are mostly about teaching. So for each root I give the **body span** plus the **Follow-up span**. The heavier math behind several roots sits in appendix files (`deriv-recursive-update`, `deriv-sector-condition`, `deriv-gain-sector`, `deriv-fisher-local-update-gain`, `deriv-tempo-additivity`), so I pair each body with its appendix.
2. **The theory's global assumptions sit outside its own segments.** GA-1 (fresh noise), GA-2/GA-2S (Model D / Model S disturbance), GA-3 (the sector condition), GA-4 and GA-5 are defined only in a table in `NOTATION.md:250-263`. No segment carries them. A rebuild probably wants them as first-class records.
3. **Some root findings recorded in the gold were never landed in the bodies.** Two I confirmed by reading `HEAD`:
   - `post-causal-structure.md:37` still says "Nominal coupling" for a case that is *inside* agency scope. `scope-agency` uses "nominal agents" for the case *outside* it, so the same word means opposite things. The gold at `post-causal-structure.md:69` calls this a certified finding.
   - `scope-agency.md:25` still states the Part I agency scope using Pearl's $do(\cdot)$. The operator's home segment, `def-pearl-causal-hierarchy`, sits in Part II and is not in `scope-agency`'s `depends:`. The gold at `scope-agency.md:73` records a way to strengthen this rather than soften it: state condition 4 in the Part I primitives $T$ and $h$, which makes Part I provably independent of the causal machinery.

   Treat the Follow-up subsections as a partly unworked backlog of findings against the roots, not as resolved history. Some items are marked resolved and verified in place; many are not.
4. **The epistemic labels disagree with each other at the roots.** This is the "a surface claims more than what's under it" pattern your orientation log names, found right at the foundation:
   - `emp-update-gain` is `type: empirical`, yet its Epistemic Status (`:42`) says "Derived under the Fisher-local invariance regime."
   - `def-mismatch-signal` is `status: axiomatic`, but its body calls it "definitional" (FU `:74`).
   - `def-adaptive-tempo` is a definition with `status: conditional`.
   - `form-objective-functional` is a `formulation` with `status: axiomatic`, which auditors flagged as overstating (see `scope-agent-identity.md:94`).
   - `der-recursive-update` has an open disagreement over `exact` versus `conditional` (`deriv-recursive-update.md:236-238`).

   The labeling system has four overlapping layers (`type`, `status`, the equation tag, and `stage`), and that system is itself a design question for v3.
5. **Some structural tensions at the root are visible from this patch alone.** These are pointers, not conclusions:
   - **IB versus directed separation.** Information-bottleneck policy-relativity says the optimal $M_t$ depends on future actions (`form-information-bottleneck.md:50`, `def-model-sufficiency.md:47`). Part II's directed separation says $f_M$ is goal-blind. The two are reconciled only by a continuation-policy convention $\pi_{\text{cont}}$ that is defined later, in Part II (`def-value-object.md:35-37`). The gold names this "the very first crack in the armor of Directed Separation" (`form-information-bottleneck.md:93`).
   - **"Completeness" bundles two different properties** (`der-recursive-update.md:71`), and **priors / $M_0$ have no place** in $M_t = \phi(\mathcal C_t)$ (`form-agent-model.md:62`, `def-chronica.md:87`).
   - **Two different capacities share one word:** $R$ (the dynamical basin) and $\mathcal F(\mathcal M)$ (the representational ceiling) (`impl-persistence-and-limits.md:100`).
   - **The scope predicate $H(\Omega_t \mid \mathcal C_t) \gt 0$ has no temporal quantifier** (`scope-adaptive-system.md:69`).
   - **"Agent" names both the umbrella and the agency tier** (`scope-adaptive-system.md:70`).
   - **The rate ladder confused readers** ($\eta^\ast$ per event, then $\mathcal T = \nu\eta^\ast$, then $\alpha_{\text{time}} = \nu\eta^\ast c_{\min}$). It was repaired on 2026-05-30 (`der-gain-sector-bridge.md:41-45`), and the chapter's misquotation catalog (`impl-persistence-and-limits.md:109-113`) shows readers still slip on it.
6. **The newest foundational work is in Part II, not Part I.** `deriv-decomposition-uniqueness` (2026-08-24, the last commits to Volume 1) asks whether the $(M_t, G_t)$ split is forced. Its answer: the split is underdetermined by directed separation alone, and canonical only relative to an explicitly chosen family of goal-interventions. That makes it a root of Part II and of everything that cites $X_t = (M_t, G_t)$. I placed it high.
7. **"The roots" probably reach into Part II's opening chapter.** By `depends:` counts, the single Volume 1 segment that Volumes 2–4 lean on most is `der-directed-separation`: 10 files depend on it and 11 cite it. Next come `der-orient-cascade`, `def-chronica`, `scope-agent-identity`, `result-persistence-condition` and `form-complete-agent-state`. A foundation change to the $X_t$ lift propagates further than most Part I changes, so I interleaved that lift with late Part I material rather than putting it after all of Part I.
8. **Most of the figures are orphaned.** Only five figure sources are referenced anywhere in canon: `driver-snow-foundation`, `scope-of-work`, `bathtub-scaffold`, `orient-cascade` and `strategy-dag-example`. The twelve small SVGs (`agent-environment.svg`, `persistence-condition.svg`, …) are referenced only from audits and spikes. The `dep-graph-*.dot/.svg` files were last regenerated 2026-05-15 and are stale against the current outline. Joseph's own comment on them is at `01-aat-core/OUTLINE.md:15`.
9. **Outline and file hygiene.**
   - `src/old-tf-appendix-f-multi-agent.md` is the only segment file missing from `OUTLINE.md`, yet `def-causal-information-yield.md:55` still cites it as "source material."
   - The OUTLINE row `worked-example-cam` points at a file that does not exist.
   - The Part II preface cites `#result-section-ii-survival`, which actually lives in `03-llm-core`.
   - `01-aat-core/aat.aux` (1,501 lines) is a LaTeX build cache, not content.
10. **The first attempt already walked part of this ground (pointer only; another finder covers it).** `audits/aat-reboot-aux-supplemental/` (17 files, 2026-09-25) is the first attempt's per-segment supplementary notes on INTRODUCTION and the first 16 Part I segments, through `der-action-selection`. It maps one-to-one onto entries 1–24 below. Nothing from `def-mismatch-signal` onward got that close, foundation-oriented walk.
11. **Coverage, stated honestly.** Volume 1's `src/` is about 4.3 MB (roughly 1M tokens), more than one finder can read whole.
    - **Part I chapters 1–3:** bodies read in full, plus their Follow-up items.
    - **Chapter 4 and the core Part I appendices:** read compactly (summary, formal tags and section map, Epistemic Status, Follow-ups).
    - **Part II's lift chapter:** summaries, Epistemic Status, section maps, plus targeted reads of `der-directed-separation` and `deriv-decomposition-uniqueness`.
    - **The rest of Part II, Part III and the remaining appendices:** first paragraph, labels and section map only.

    The ordering is therefore best-informed at the top and coarser below roughly entry 45. Below that line, treat the order as provisional.
