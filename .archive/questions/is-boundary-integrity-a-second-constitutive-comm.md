# Is boundary integrity a second constitutive commitment beside the information-loss boundary? The coupling names one commitment (the agent cannot read Omega directly) and relies everywhere on a second it never states: Omega cannot write to the agent's internal state. M_t = phi(C_t) presumes M_t's only inputs are the chronica's; the mismatch signal presumes o-hat_t was generated by the agent's own model; f_M's arguments are the ones its type names. Under a permeable boundary these are not well-typed. Two cases where permeability is real: prompt injection / weight tampering (adversarial), and a context-window agent whose outputs are its next observations (native). Conjecture attached, marked as such: boundary permeability and GUC Class 3 coupling may be one fact seen from two angles.
