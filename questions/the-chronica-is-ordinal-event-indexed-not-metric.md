# The chronica is ordinal (event-indexed), not metric. Two implications are flagged but not modeled: (1) a suspended agent's temporal gap is invisible at the sequence level but appears in the mismatch signal on resumption (awakening protocols); (2) agents with very different event rates sharing one Omega have diverging subjective time, and cross-tempo coupling is not prescribed. Where do these land: form-event-driven-dynamics, the logogenic volume, or auxilia composition? Source: ASF def-chronica Working Notes (audit 04-def-chronica §14).
