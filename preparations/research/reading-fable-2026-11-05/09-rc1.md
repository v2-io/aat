## 00-OUTLINE and 01-SPINE (read whole)

Surprises against expectation:
- **"Foundation × exercised" plane.** I did not have this from any secondhand source. "What grounds it … is set at conception and predicts only the probability of being right. What has been *done* to it is accumulated by acts." And minting "derives the record's securing lines, one per declared foundation rung, because *the instrument follows the ground*: grounded in proof → derive it; in declaration → ratify it; in reality → measure it; in a proven model → check the instance; in analogy → break the analogy". This is about *what builds and supports* a piece of content, which is what Joseph said today RC1 downplays. It is there, at altitude 2–3, but subordinated: the *definition* of kind is failure-and-repair, and foundation enters as a declared attribute at minting. PLAN's "position vs credence" keeps "exercised" (position) and largely loses "foundation" as a declared thing that *generates* the lines.
- **"One atom, of one kind."** RC1 does use "atom". And "Every record … is a small cluster: a body …; working notes carrying forward-work only; an append-only trail; projections computed from the trail — every status cell, every outline row; companions as needed." So in RC1 the record IS the segment-sized thing with working notes attached, and at the same time "one atom … individuated by failure-and-repair". The conflation Joseph named is in the spine's own two adjacent paragraphs: unit of failure = the thing with a body, working notes and companions.
- **Projection** here = anything computed from the trail: "every status cell, every outline row". Joseph in the dialog: "for many months now was the term for … something between a manifestation and a group". An outline row as a projection fits his sense; "status cell" is the computed-summary sense. RC1 holds both under one word already.
- Namespaces: "the namespace is part of the name, never an object … any namespace-level value a consumer sees is a declared projection, never a stored field." So "Evidence" is not a dimension; `evidence/derivation` is. A roll-up across them is a declared projection. That is the *aliased/derived* instance-dimension idea, one level down: roll-ups are declared, never stored.
- The fused-dimension tell: "a ladder whose values are unrolled permutations of independently-movable parts … is two dimensions sharing a field, and the cross-product longhand is the proof". Compare the ASF outline's "Derived + Scope", "Discussion + Hypothesis": cross-product longhand at kind level. Same tell, one level up, says those are two things sharing a slug.
- "Composite kinds are dimension-compositions, not new dimensions: a definition is a coinage (Backing) plus fit-to-usage (Evidence)". So RC1's answer to multi-part things is to compose *dimensions* on one record. No parts with their own dimensions. That is the gap the template fills.
- The acceptance test: "a fresh auditor, given nothing but the records and their carried states, knows exactly what to check." Keep. It is a test of the model that does not depend on any of its terms.
- Order-invariance of aggregation; "anchoring is that race condition in cognitive form". Keep.
- "Standing … a gate, not a dimension: a record produced without the power to produce it never existed as its kind."
- "Altitude 1" three consequences. Number 2: "Honesty is only well-defined relative to kind." I think this survives translation: honesty is only well-defined relative to *what the content does*, which is now a property of the leaf's kind, and the whole's honesty is a declared function of its parts'.

Question 1 (is "not meaningful alone" in RC1?): not so far. The spine speaks of records one at a time; relations are "dependents re-price".

## 02-RECORD-OBJECT-MODEL and 03-DIMENSIONS (read whole)

**RC1 contradicts itself on grain, and the contradiction is the knot.**
- 02 RECORD invariant: "The adjudication grain and the record grain coincide — a record carrying two independently-adjudicable things is two records."
- 03 admission table, row Grain: "**machinery** (parameter) — *every* dimension can attach below the record (per-clause status, per-clause deciders are live practice); a parameter of dimensions, not one of them".
- 03 Working Notes: "Open: Grain's projection rule upward (headline vs floor) is unresolved estate-wide".
If every dimension can attach below the record, the adjudication grain does not coincide with the record grain. RC1 saw sub-record attachment as "live practice", called it a parameter, and left the roll-up rule open. Joseph's templates are the answer to that open item: the roll-up is *declared per whole* (aliased to one part / derived from parts / own). And 02's RECORD is `has → BODY {1,1}, WORKING-NOTES {0,1}, TRAIL {1,1}, COMPANION {0,N}`: one body. One body with per-clause status is the thing with no name.

So my expectation "no notion of a multi-part declared shape" was right about the object model and wrong about awareness: RC1 names the pressure three times (grain row; "inner keyed elements are inner `@{location}`s able to hold bindings of their own (this is where sub-record grain attaches)"; the open projection-upward rule) and resolves none.

**Things to keep, now seen at the primary:**
- DIMENSION as "the unit of orthogonality … one independently-movable value with its own field, its own ladder, and its own movers, accumulating its own subrecords". The steward's three-part articulation: a question; degrees of warrant; *its dishonesty modes* ("the specific ways a record can report a position it doesn't occupy on this question"). PLAN's four quantities keep the first two and drop the third. The dishonesty modes per dimension are attested from Joseph's bursts for Evidence, Backing, Fidelity and are the most hard-won content in the file.
- "a record's epistemic state is a *vector*, and any scalar shown to a consumer is a declared projection of it."
- LINE: "one record's accumulating engagement with one dimension over time … holding that dimension's subrecords as they accrue." PLAN has evidence events and no line. In the new model a line is (leaf × dimension) over time. "A claim's Evidence-family line-set derives from its declared foundation rungs."
- LADDER: "The ceiling is assignable from kind before work, and names the action that would raise the record toward it."
- PROJECTION: "A projection is declared (strongest-line | every-line-sustains | shown-independent-agreement | …); one unqualified scalar is not a default." and "evaluated … as of a moment. There is no untimed evaluation." The declared-projection set is exactly the vocabulary a template's derived dimensions need (min / headline / floor / every-member-sustains).
- **Bounds**: usage license, claim license, audience-safety, conflict precedence. "declared, consulted-at-a-moment, not warranted by degrees". Neither PLAN nor the model has them. A claim license ("what a record may assert of its own: none / summarize / generalize / original") is precisely what distinguishes the impl segment's "this segment's own framing" clauses from its inherited ones.
- Standing as gate; publication membrane as gate.
- Under-recording as the cardinal sin's mirror, with "deliberate non-lifts are recorded as decline-events with reasons, so absence stays meaning *unrun*".
- "organization keeps trying to become ontology; the name-level cut is the form that structurally can't." A warning that applies to templates: a template bears invariants (required slots, roll-up rules), so it IS ontology and must earn that. A mere grouping for convenience should not be a template.

**Things I think are mediocre or wrong for our purposes:**
- KIND = speech-act individuated by failure-and-repair, with "kinds as dimension-compositions" as the whole account of compound things. It makes "definition" one record with Backing + Evidence dimensions. It cannot say that the coinage and the usage-fit are about *different pieces of content* (the name; the statement of meaning).
- "Fidelity is the substrate, not a peer" and "Disposition is the bus" inside a table of peers: two of eight rows are not dimensions in the same sense as the others, and the file says so and keeps them in the table.
- The record "is a small cluster" with working notes as a part: `Open: whether WORKING-NOTES is an object or a body-part with editable-free semantics`. Still open there too. And "crumb-routing as the working-notes → trail drain" is a to-do. So the per-record inbox has been an unplaced mechanism since August.

**On question 1** (not meaningful alone): still absent. Records are adjudicated one at a time. "Dimensions legitimately inform one another through recorded events" is about dimensions, not about neighbouring content.
**On question 2** (projection): "any computed summary over trails: a status cell, an outline row, a composite strength." Both senses under one word, confirmed.

## 04-CYCLE and 06-EDGES (read whole)

**The "what builds it" half is here, and it is good.** MINT: "**foundation declared** — which rungs of ground this record can stand on (provable? measurable in an authored world? in the wild? an instance of an established model? witnessed? carried from outside?). … it derives which `evidence/*` dimensions this record's securing work opens, and a dimension not declared correctly never opens (nor silently pretends to)." So in RC1 a kind does not fix the dimension set; the *declared foundation of this particular record* opens a subset. That is a per-content declaration of how it is to be built. PLAN flattened this into "kinds and their aspects". The model has "atom kind declares 1..N dimensions". Neither carries "this content declares its ground, and the ground opens the lines". I think this belongs in the model: a leaf has a kind (which dimensions are *possible*) and a declared foundation (which are *open*), and an unopened dimension is a positive fact, not an absence.
- "analogy deliberately has **no row**: it grounds ideation … never assertion."
- The worked ladders, with refutation outcomes per line and ceiling laws: derivation (sketch → drafted → deps-verified → re-derived; refuted by counterexample → no-go; ceiling "exact under named premises; conditional if any premise is empirical"); in-vivo; wild-empirical; instance ("mapping breaks under perturbation → scope-bound (narrows, doesn't fail)"; "inherits the model's tier, on the verified overlap only"); testimony; carriage ("min(carriage rung, source's own tier)"). ASF's `stage:` values (draft / deps-verified / claims-verified) are visibly a worn-down copy of the derivation ladder applied to whole files.
- `decision/*` with attested semantics for supported / ratified / ruled, and structural cross-dimension guards: "*ratified* requires grounding-explicit **and** consultation-announced; *ruled* deliberately carries no grounding guard". A guard that reads two dimensions of one thing. In template terms the same shape appears across parts: a whole's dimension guarded on its parts' values.
- LAND on refutation: "the record's *identity persists*; its *body* is replaced by the present truth (the no-go, the narrowed scope — the refuted content deleted, never kept-softened); its *trail* keeps the whole history — so `supports` edges into the record stay resolvable and carry the cascade". Compare PLAN A1's OBO rule ("a changed meaning gets a new identifier"; "narrowed = new claim"). These disagree. RC1: same identity, new body, edges keep resolving and carry the cascade. PLAN: new record, `supersedes`. RC1's version is what makes "what is still inconsistent" computable from inbound references; PLAN's version silently detaches dependents unless they re-point. I lean RC1 here, and note Joseph's dialog treats softening as a change *to an atom* in place.

**Edges.**
- "an edge kind earns its place exactly when some consuming operation routes it differently". Keep as the admission rule for reference-dimension kinds.
- **rebut vs undercut** is exactly Joseph's latent-ladder-level point from the dialog, already worked: "B refuted → … rebut-class cascade: the securings of A that *used* B reset; if B was a necessary premise, A is marked known-broken *in the record, immediately, visibly*". "B weakened … → an undercut: A's conclusion is not attacked; A's projections that counted B at the old strength now compute lower, and where the drop crosses a serving threshold, a re-secure question mints." And point 1: "Because every status is a computed projection, re-pricing is not a mutation sweep … What re-pricing actually requires is notification and threshold detection". The dialog's "ceilings propagate by rule; dependents above their ceiling go on the frontier" is this, restated. "known-broken in the record, immediately, visibly" is also the known-defect mark Joseph was pondering. RC1 already had it for the rebut case.
- "partial dependence (A uses one clause of B) requires grain on the edge — `supports` at clause grain — which the object model's grain parameter covers but no instantiation has exercised." The dialog's "an introduction can rest on another instance's eli8 atom specifically" is that exercise.
- "strength never propagates transitively (support chains attenuate…); reachability is transitive for notification only". `seeded-by` is the one transitive edge. `restates` exact vs near. `narrates` not transitive.
- **Intended cardinality on the reference**, "what gives a miss its meaning": zero against {1,1} = the declarer's model is wrong; zero against {0,N} = just the current answer. Slot cardinality in a template is the same idea for containment.
- **Designator vs description**: a reference that consults a maintained binding vs one that runs a test at use ("every record citing X") and "fails through the world's motion". A template slot filled "by query" (all segments with tag T) is a description; filled by listed names is designators. Canonical groupings will have both.
- "Each edge is itself a small record: declarer, date, and — where contested — a minted question … But edges deliberately do *not* get the full dimension apparatus — … over-building here would recreate the mixed-composite pathology one level down." This is in tension with the dialog's "dependency is an array dimension on the claim atom". Not a contradiction: RC1 says a reference has a declarer, a date and can be contested; the dialog says where it lives. But "does `predicated-on` itself have standing (is it *true* that A needs B)?" is a real question. RC1 says: minimal, contest by minting a question.

**On question 1:** LAND says "Collisions surface and block 'done'. Dependents re-price." Still about pairs across records. Nothing about parts of one thing needing each other to be meaningful.
**On question 4:** support is an edge with declarer/date; ceilings: carriage "min(carriage rung, source's own tier)"; instance "inherits the model's tier, on the verified overlap only". Ceiling transmission exists per line, not as a general rule along `supports`.

## 07, 08, 09 (read whole)

## Overall: what RC1 is, read whole

Joseph, mid-read: "See how it got real close and did some good reductionist work but didn't quite see the whole picture?" Yes, and I can now say where, specifically.

**RC1 is a theory of one piece of content under one question at a time.** Record × dimension × event. Everything it does well lives there: what a dimension is (a question, degrees of warrant, its dishonesty modes); that dimensions never substitute; that status is computed from recorded acts as of a moment; that the declared ground opens the lines; how independent lines combine (coherence default) and how they may touch (commitment law); what a refutation leaves behind; what a certificate is and where warrant bottoms out; that under-recording is as dishonest as over-claiming. Those are the "constraints" in grok's line, and they are the product.

**What it does not have is any unit above the piece, in either direction it is needed.**
- *In structure:* no whole that pieces belong to. Compounds are handled by (a) composing dimensions on one record, or (b) cutting into separate records joined by edges, or (c) "cluster", one name with typed inner parts that carry no state. The roll-up rule ("Grain's projection rule upward") is listed open in 03, asked for in Q7, and has no declaration form in 09. The August strain is Joseph hitting this.
- *In time:* no act that spans pieces. Events are per record. The field trial found this from outside (EPISODE).

These are the same omission. A whole in structure is what gets written together; a write that spans parts is what makes them a whole. Today's dialog supplies both at once and ties them: the instance is the structural whole, the instance-isolated changeset is the temporal one, and each defines the other. I think that tie is the actual new idea, more than the words atom and template.

**And one framing choice hid the gap:** defining the unit by how it *fails*. Failure is a per-piece, per-dimension matter, so a theory organized around it will always cut finer and never ask what the pieces are *for*. Organized around support (what this rests on, inside its whole and outside), the whole appears immediately, because support inside a segment is dense and support across segments is sparse, and that difference in density is what a segment *is*.

## Take / leave, for the model (my judgment; to be argued)

**Take, nearly as is:** dimension (three-part definition, orthogonality, vector, fusion test, substitution law); declared foundation opens the lines, per piece; event tuple, trail, status as a computation as of a moment, order-invariance; the *declared projection set* (strongest-line / every-line-sustains / shown-independent-agreement) as the vocabulary for roll-ups; coherence default, commitment law, transmission as calibrated ambient prior; rebut vs undercut, re-pricing as recompute + notify + threshold, known-broken shown immediately; references with intended cardinality, designator vs description, dangle and collide, identity as a maintained binding, inner locations; bounds (usage license, claim license, audience-safety, conflict precedence) and gates (standing, publication); disposition as placement-for-influence with a declared closer and two signs of influence; decline events and the expressive requirement; certification and the trusted base; the auditor-walk acceptance test; the worked-specimen form.

**Leave:** "record" as the unit of fate and its one-body anatomy; kind-as-speech-act as the organizing idea, and "kinds as dimension-compositions" as the account of compound things; the boundary test as an organizing principle (keep it as a cutting tool); the four closure shapes as a list (they fall out of named / unnamed-inside-named / generated / outside); Fidelity and Disposition presented as peers of the other dimensions.

**Unresolved there and still unresolved here:** `@{moment}`; whether events are themselves addressable things; where the per-thing inbox lives ("whether WORKING-NOTES is an object or a body-part"); thresholds for when a recomputed drop demands attention.
