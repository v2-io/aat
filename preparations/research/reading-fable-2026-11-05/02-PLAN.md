## What surprised me

- "Nothing is inferred from anything else" (A0). Position, credence, judged value, graduation level, each separately recorded/computed and never standing in for one another. This is the substitution law generalized. It makes the model wide: every aspect value is potentially four things plus a basis plus a graduation level.
- "A warrant is itself a truth claim, with a credence." and graduation level 3: the aspect becomes a record with aspects starting at level 0. So aspect values are *themselves potential records*. Under an atom/template/instance vocabulary: is an aspect value an atom? If it can be refuted separately (it can: "this is stale" can be wrong while the content is fine), it has its own fate. By PLAN's own definition of record, every aspect value is a record at graduation 0. The plan avoids saying so.
- Upward lapse is a *declared rule*, not automatic, and "checks bind to what they directly read". This is the mechanism that makes sectioned composites workable. It also means a template must be able to say what a whole-review *reads* (text of parts? standing of parts? headline only?).
- The 245-segment reach and 0.95^20: the far corners "will compute low, honestly". Either that is a true and uncomfortable fact about ASF, or per-link credence for mathematics must be ≥0.99, or the conjunction model is wrong for shared-definition theories (positive dependence). The plan serves the interval. Fine. But note this is the place where "grain" changes the numbers: at statement grain the chain is longer than at segment grain, so finer atoms compute *lower* far-corner credence under independence. Grain is not neutral for the mathematics.
- "the sprint's 313 findings have no kind field". "Typed findings are a precondition, still owed."
