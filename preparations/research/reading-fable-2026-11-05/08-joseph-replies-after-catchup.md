**On "unit of fate":**
> the unit-of-fate / what it takes to invalidate is an RC1 spine. It has always seemed a little bit wrong to me but I have had a hard time seeing how. Now I realize it's because it was conflating a core dimension which determines how a piece of content "fails" -- but most of the time that has distracted from the reality that most of the time when building this system we have to think in terms of what *builds* or supports a particular piece of content, and it downplays […] the fact that almost no atom at that level of granularity is meaningful on its own and that in most verisectoria their fates are intertwined in complex ways but usually such that if a core part of a group of atoms fails-- they all go down with it potentially

**On terminology and the old documents:**
