# Appendix A to PLAN.md: candidate record and composite kinds for the new ASF store

*First iteration, 2026-11-05, councils-46 with Joseph. **Not yet provisional.** It's a list to argue with, not a list to build from. Nothing here is a definition. By PLAN.md's anchor rule, ASF's kinds are admitted only from specimens (step 6: the five root segments, crossed through v2), each justified by the founding questions. This list is an input to that step and may not survive it.*

*Vocabulary is PLAN.md's: position, credence (P(true)), judged value, forecast (A0); aspects sorted by basis (evidenced / decided / attested), attributes, rules (A2); composites, arrangements, and the three graphs (A1).*

---

## What's in doubt about this list as a whole

**1. It may not be a partition.** The rows are written as if every record is exactly one kind. Several look more like facets a record can carry at once:
- a definition is a coinage (decided), and it also fits usage (evidenced);
- a segment is a composite, and also has an arrangement;
- a finding is a defect report, and also an open item.

A better set-theoretic footing is wanted before any of this is declared. Are kinds disjoint classes, or intersections of facets, or types with roles? Joseph, 2026-11-05: *"I'm not sure if any of this is quite as well-defined as I'd like — it seems like there are still some conflations and it might need some better set-theory perspective."*

**2. The small pieces are coming together; the macro-structure isn't.** Joseph: *"it feels like it's coming together in the smaller pieces and substructure, but its macro-structure and real practical flow is still wanting."* The list says what records might be. It doesn't yet say how work actually flows through them in ASF day to day. Working Notes (below) is the clearest example of a practical mechanism the list doesn't yet capture.

**3. Some bases in the table are guesses.** Where a row says *evidenced* or *decided*, that is the first reading, not a derivation.

## Working Notes: a mechanism the list doesn't yet hold

In current ASF, a segment's **Working Notes** section is, in Joseph's words, *"one of the most effective (when used correctly) higher-level mechanisms in ASF … that allows the accumulation of 'misc' and that is an influx — often referencing other segments and pieces etc. — for a specific segment to be drained."*

Read that way, Working Notes is a **segment-local influx**: a membrane at segment grain. Anything bearing on that segment collects there, from any source, and is drained into that segment's records or delivered elsewhere. `def-agent-environment`'s Working Notes hold six different fates under one heading (PLAN A1). That isn't a failure of the mechanism. It's exactly what an influx holds before crossing.

Candidate reading, not decided: every composite (segment, chapter, perhaps part) has its own local influx. Its items are chunks with a target composite. Draining it is part of that composite's episodes. The global influx and the local ones are the same mechanism at different grains. If that holds, it answers part of the macro-structure gap: work arrives at the composite it concerns, and stays visible there until it's crossed.
