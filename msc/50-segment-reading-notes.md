## Ch.1 The Coupled Loop

### 1. `def-agent-environment` (read whole earlier; body 79–113)

- Commits: Ω as totality of external state; three channels (perception / internal state / action) as inventory; **information-loss boundary** as the constitutive commitment ("not a simplifying assumption but a scope condition"); "agent" as umbrella vs tier labels.
- Record-boundary test: at least three independently-failable pieces — (a) the channel inventory (definition), (b) the info-loss boundary (a *scope* commitment, truth-apt only as "this is the regime we analyze"), (c) the claim "for full-access systems the adaptive machinery becomes vacuous" (a truth-apt claim — and arguably false as stated: full *state* access with unknown *law* still needs learning; see SP-30 / aat-refactored typed target. Coherence with those, since I'd read them).
- WN shows the "boundary integrity" candidate (Ω can't write internal state) — a missing second constitutive commitment; plus "computational opacity vs perceptual opacity". Both are exactly the kind of root-level gap a foundation refactor should decide, not park.

### 2. `def-action-transition` (1–68: body + gold incl. Follow-ups)

- Commits: action space $\mathcal A$; $\Omega_{t+1} \sim T(\cdot\mid\Omega_t,a_t)$; **transition opacity** ("the agent does not know $T$ exactly"); Markov-in-Ω as a *breadth* commitment ("Ω is the sufficient state for its own evolution"), paralleled with the M-side C3 move.
- Record boundaries: (a) the definition of $\mathcal A$, $T$; (b) transition opacity — a scope/postulate, not a definition (three auditors disagreed on its tag: the eq-tag taxonomy has no home for constitutive-scope claims — a *kind-system* gap, exactly what RC1's failure-and-repair individuation should settle); (c) the Markov-breadth formulation (a choice with an alternative, and a stated parallel to C3 — itself a cross-record claim, "the two are independent"); (d) the truth-apt claim "if $T$ were known, action selection would reduce to optimization over a known function" — overstated as written when $h$ is still lossy: known $T$ + known $h$ + hidden $\Omega_t$ is still a belief-tracking (filtering) problem; what known $T$ removes is *law* learning, not state tracking. The body's "the joint opacity of $h$ and $T$ is what creates the need for adaptive behavior" conflates the two. (Coherence with aat-refactored's state/law/chance/compute typing and SP-30; I note it because v3's root must type the target before this sentence can be true.)
- Gold worth keeping as a forward question, not parked: the **bounded/unbounded Markov asymmetry** (Ω-side augmentation is WLOG; M-side augmentation "has teeth" — cost relocates to capacity). And the **unstated learnability/smoothness assumption on $T$** (the theory needs *some* exploitable regularity, never named) — in RC1 terms a missing scope record.
- v3 observation: a "definition" segment here carries ~4 records of 3 different kinds (definition / scope / formulation / claim). This will be the norm at the roots. A segment-per-file grain loses this; a card-per-statement grain or named inner statements keeps it.

### 3. `def-observation-function` (1–44 body; 55–66 gold incl. Follow-ups)

- Commits: $o_t = h(\Omega_t, a_{t-1}, \varepsilon_t)$; *lossy* $h$ (constitutive); **epistemic opacity** ("knows neither $h$ nor the distribution of $\varepsilon_t$ exactly") — called "scope-defining choices", tagged `*[Definition]*`.
- Record boundaries: definition of $\mathcal O$, $h$ (with optional active-perception arg) · lossiness (scope) · epistemic opacity (scope) · the claim "this information loss is what later forces the agent to maintain an internal model" (truth-apt; again, *state* hiddenness alone forces a model — opacity of the *law* is a separate commitment).
- **The Kalman tension is a root-level kind problem, not a wording problem:** epistemic opacity ("unknown $h$ and noise law") is *stronger* than what the theory's own calibration anchor (`example-kalman`, known $H$, $R$) satisfies. Four auditors converged on it. Either Kalman is out of scope (absurd for the anchor) or the opacity clause is not constitutive. The honest fix is the typed target (state vs law vs chance): AAT's constitutive commitment is *inaccessibility of Ω_t*; opacity of $h$/$T$/noise law is an *optional, per-scope* commitment. This is the same seam as entry 2's (d). Three segments in a row carry the same un-typed "unknown" → v3 should state the typed target once at the root (SP-30's package exists — read `asf/spikes/epistemic-target-ontology/` before drafting anything here).
- Is $a_{t-1}$ in $h$ ever used downstream? (open, gold) — a v3 "edge with intended cardinality" check would answer it mechanically: does any record `supports:` on the active-perception clause?
- "Constitutive-opacity triad" (gold) — three segments state one commitment three ways with no naming paragraph. v3: one record, three clauses, or one scope record cited three times.

### 4. `def-chronica` (1–39 body; 40–66 open-question WN; 83–90 Follow-ups)

- Commits, in one "definition": (a) the interleaved sequence $\mathcal C_t$; (b) the ordering reflects an irreversible physical fact (truth-apt; a *time* commitment — aat-refactored made it the loop's "time clause"); (c) monotone growth — "never removed" (a claim about which object? true of the *history*, false of any finite *store*); (d) "the *only* raw material from which the agent can ever construct a model" (an empiricist boundary — excludes priors / $M_0$ / pretrained weights / evolved structure; the gold's strongest root Follow-up, and Vol 3–4's face of the same gap); (e) non-forkability and continuity persistence (a cross-record claim pointing forward to `scope-agent-identity`; wording "duplicating an agent's *state*" is wrong — $M_t$ *is* copyable; the singular object is the trajectory); (f) ordinal-not-metric indexing (consequential — "waking in the dark", heterogeneous tempo).
- My own read: this segment silently identifies three different objects — the *history* (what happened at the boundary; append-only by nature), the agent's *retention* (what it holds; bounded, lossy), and an external *record* (archives/logs read back through the channel). Every claim above is true of exactly one of them. aat-refactored's "history / retention / record" trichotomy (scratch/04 — coherence, I'd read about it) names this; Vol 3's "chronica severed at every session" vs this segment's "never removed" is the predictable collision. A v3 root needs the three as three definitions.

### 5. `post-causal-structure` (1–50 body; 64–73 gold incl. Follow-ups)

### 6. `scope-adaptive-system` (1–49 body; 64–72 gold incl. Follow-ups) — `stage: claims-verified`

- My own read, before checking SP-30's text (I know only its title, "fix `#scope-adaptive-system`'s false exclusion-rationale; scope in standard RL" — so this is coherence with the title, not independent): the predicate is $H(\Omega_t\mid\mathcal C_t) \gt 0$ — residual uncertainty about the *state*. The exclusion rationale says $H = 0$ means "complete knowledge of the environment … optimal control over known dynamics is a solved problem". Those are different things. A fully observed MDP with an **unknown transition law** (tabular RL; most of standard RL) has $H(\Omega_t\mid\mathcal C_t) = 0$ at every $t$ — it is *excluded* from AAT's broadest scope by this predicate, though it is the paradigm learning agent. The predicate types the target as "state"; the rationale reasons about "law". Same un-typed "unknown" as entries 2–3, now at the scope boundary where it changes membership. In RC1 terms: a scope record whose *rationale* (a decision/grounding) is false while its *predicate* (a definition) is well-formed — two records, failing apart.
- Other independently-failable claims inside: "sufficient for mismatch / gain / tempo / persistence / all of Part I" (truth-apt sufficiency claim — persistence additionally needs disturbance assumptions GA-2 and sector conditions; overstated); "pure computation has no agent-environment boundary" (arguable — the gold's computational-opacity note); the cascade claim to agency (forward).
- Follow-ups worth carrying into v3 as decided questions, not WN: temporal quantifier on $H \gt 0$ (∀t / inf / running-with-ρ>0); God's-eye predicate vs the agent's estimate (*perspective* again — evaluated by the modeler over the true Ω); "Agent" in the set-builder for passive systems.
- **`stage: claims-verified` and `status: axiomatic` on a segment carrying a false exclusion rationale** — the label layer certifies what the content doesn't support; FORMAT's own Gate 2 ("labeling a formulation choice as `exact`…") was nominally passed.

### 7. `scope-agency` (1–52 body; 68–75 gold incl. Follow-ups) — `stage: claims-verified`

- **The Formal Expression and the Discussion define different scopes.** Condition 4 (formal) is $\exists a\neq a' : P(o\mid do(a)) \neq P(o\mid do(a'))$ — satisfied by *either* channel (Ω-routed effect, or pure active perception via $h$'s $a_{t-1}$ argument). The Discussion's "channel distinction" paragraph then says "the operative contrast is the Ω-routed one", puts pure active perception "at the boundary", and adds observability ("an Ω-affecting action whose effect never surfaces through $h$ is equally outside"). The body's best content (and the one downstream results actually use) lives in Discussion prose, not in the predicate. Under v3's "teaching never supports" rule, either the predicate is restated (world-routed, observable contrast — aat-refactored's "Option A", Joseph 2026-04-01 per its scratch/06; the "three action targets: world / channel / self") or downstream results cite the wrong record. A clean specimen of formal-layer-lags-prose.
- Quantifiers missing: contrast at *which* Ω (some reachable state? all?), at *which* times; ∃-one-pair (gold notes 100 nominal + 1 effective still passes — fine, but downstream may assume more).
- `do(·)` used before it's defined and absent from `depends:` — certified finding, unlanded; the strengthen route (state it in $T$, $h$) is recorded in WN and would make Part I independent of Part II machinery.
- Truth-apt claims riding in a scope: "they can model, but they cannot learn causal structure or rationally plan against it" (for passive/nominal) — true of *interventional* causal structure; passive systems can learn some causal structure observationally under assumptions. Overstated.

## Ch.1 as a whole — what it shows for v3 (my synthesis, 2026-10-28; hypothesis register)

1. **Every Ch.1 segment is a composite of 3–6 independently-failable statements of 3–4 kinds** (definition, scope, formulation/choice, truth-apt claim, norm, cross-record claim). ASF's segment-per-file grain + one `type:`/`status:` per file makes most of them unaddressable; the audits had to quote sentences. This is the strongest first-hand support so far for card/statement grain (spec-lite's "one MUST = one card"; RC1 grain invariant; 09-29 "names for every assertion").
2. **One root seam recurs in five of seven segments: the un-typed "unknown".** State hiddenness, law opacity, noise-law opacity, and (by the scope predicate) residual *state* entropy are used interchangeably; the scope predicate then excludes fully-observed RL while the rationale reasons about known dynamics. SP-30's typed epistemic target is the corpus's own fix, packaged and waiting since July. **v3's first root record probably has to be the typed target** — this is a theory decision Joseph holds (SP-30 is in his queue), not a format decision.
3. **Certified findings sit unlanded in Working Notes for months** ("nominal" collision; `do` before definition; diff-voice lineage tag) — the "un-clocked queue" diagnosis (PROCESS-MAP) seen first-hand. v3 needs findings to be records with a target, a version, and a closer, not WN prose.
4. **Labels certify what content doesn't support** (`claims-verified` + `axiomatic` on a scope with a false rationale; a norm inheriting a postulate's `axiomatic`). Confirms H5 (status must be computed / earned per record, not set per file).
5. **Perspective is never declared** (modeler's God's-eye $H(\Omega_t\mid\mathcal C_t)$ vs agent's belief) — candidate root commitment for v3.
6. Not yet checked by me: whether the strongest Ch.1 content (Ω-routed agency, the trichotomy) actually lives in better form in `spikes/epistemic-target-ontology/` (SP-30 package) — **read that next, before Ch.2**, since it's the corpus's own answer to seam 2.
