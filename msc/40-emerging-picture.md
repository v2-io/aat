The aat-refactored coordinators did real theory work (Ch.2 identities verified; retention/readout split; the adaptive-scope stream form) and ran real method (cold reads, finders, verifiers, de-novo). What it lacked, by its own late admissions: status claims written by hand ("Verified" over a ~50-defect review), drafts carrying known-false statements until a status note was bolted on, an unverified taxonomy promoted into a proposal, a misattributed slogan carrying a formal clause, decisions taken under deferral with no record type that kept them arguable. - **Falsifier:** if the deep read of aat-refactored's intermediates and reviews shows the drafts' defects were mostly theory errors that SOPs would not have caught, H1 is wrong and the lesson is about theory method, not process.

## H2 — v3's founding act is a verisectorium deployment, but the template alone is the wrong seed

The template (cut 08-10) is a shell with an explicit `--GAP--` where epistemology goes. RC1 (08-14..09-01) filled that gap at model level; the 09-29 proposal mapped ASF → RC1; the spec-lite `sop/` store (09-30) instantiated a concrete version (kinds.yaml with per-kind verification ladders, three record flags, computed doc-state, ADR decisions, purpose layer). - **Falsifier:** if spec-lite's sop records turn out to be spec-specific in ways that don't transfer to a theory corpus (e.g., "rule/fixture/parser-agrees" ladders with no analog), or if Joseph's later words (STEWARD-VERBATIM, jaw-proposal) moved past them, the seed changes. ## H4 — Grain is the hardest practical question

ASF segments routinely hold several independently-failable statements (09-29 census: six of eight accepted review findings against `the-loop` landed on sentences with no name). RC1 says record grain = adjudication grain; 11 says sub-record grain attaches to inner `@{location}`s. spec-lite answered with "small cards, one normative statement each." For a theory, "one claim per card" may produce hundreds of tiny records whose reading order is the outline's job.

- **Falsifier:** if a specimen chapter (e.g., AAT Part I Ch.1) worked at card grain reads *worse* for a cold reader than ASF's current segments, the grain needs a cluster form (named inner statements inside a segment).

## H5 — Status must be computed, so v3 needs a trail before it needs tooling

Every source agrees hand-set status lies (ASF FORMAT's own `stage:` admission; RC1 01; the 09-29 census; spec-lite's "no computed columns yet"; live_docs' specimen). No vsect exists. The cheapest honest interim is probably: no status columns at all until a tool computes them (spec-lite's choice), plus an append-only event record per securing act (git commit trailers? a per-record events section? udon ledger?), plus decline-events.

- **Falsifier:** if Joseph wants status visible from day one, the interim must be a declared projection with its moment ("as of <sha>"), never a bare word.
